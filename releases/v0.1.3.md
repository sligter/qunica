# Qunica v0.1.3

- Added structured group notes with built-in templates, status and category controls, and shared authoring guidance for Agents. Existing free-form notes remain editable.
- Fixed dragging hidden workspace folders and files into the message composer.
- Made the sidebar's direct-chat and group lists scroll independently, with fixed category headers and separate lazy loading.
- Improved Git status badges for untracked, conflicted, staged, and modified files.
- Added a GitHub repository link under Settings → About.
- Expanded the documentation for group routing, scheduling, and runtime support.

## Downloads

- Android 8.0 or later: choose `android-arm64-release.apk` for most phones, or `android-x86_64-release.apk` for x86_64 devices and emulators.
- Windows: choose an installer or `portable.exe`; macOS: choose the DMG for your chip; Linux: choose the package for your architecture.
- Headless servers: choose a `qunica-server-*` archive.
